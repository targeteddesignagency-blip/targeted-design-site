### TRACKING: YOU CAN'T MANAGE WHAT YOU CAN'T MEASURE (1:05-1:35)

**[VISUAL: Dashboard mockup showing the "Single Method Trap" — a single promo code with a giant red X through it. Counter ticks up: 30%... 40%... 50% missed conversions.]**

**NARRATOR:**
Here's where most mailers fail. They print a single promo code. If you don't type it in, they assume the mail failed.

**[VISUAL: A person throws a postcard in recycling. Three days later, they open a laptop and type the company name into Google.]**

Relying on one tracking method misses 30 to 50 percent of actual conversions.

**[VISUAL: Modern tracking stack appears vertically, each lighting up as narration mentions it]**

The pros stack at least three tracking methods:

→ QR codes. Average scan rates are now 2 to 5 percent. For younger B2B audiences? 41 percent.

→ Personalized URLs — custom links unique to every recipient. 3 to 8 percent visit rate, perfectly attributed.

→ Call tracking — unique phone numbers, about 45 bucks a month, capturing the people who just pick up the phone.

**[VISUAL: "Matchback Analysis" appears as a forensic magnifying glass over a customer database]**

And after the campaign ends, they run matchback analysis — comparing the mailing list against purchases. It routinely reveals 30 to 50 percent more conversions that were completely invisible.

---

### INCREMENTALITY: THE GOLD STANDARD (1:35-1:55)

**[VISUAL: A/B test animation. 100,000 households split into two groups. One group gets nothing. The other gets the mailer.]**

**NARRATOR:**
But the gold standard — the thing that separates amateurs from pros — is incrementality testing.

You take 100,000 households. You intentionally withhold the mail from 10 to 20 percent. That's your control group.

**[VISUAL: Side-by-side comparison. Control group: 1.5% conversion. Mail group: 4% conversion. The difference highlights in green: "2.5% PROVEN LIFT"]**

If the control group converts at 1.5 percent and the mail group converts at 4 percent, you've mathematically proven a 2.5 percent incremental lift.

No guesswork. No dashboard lying to you. Just math.
