## SENDING RULES
1. Only send to leads with verified email addresses
2. Send between 9 AM and 11 AM the day before the call
3. Levi calls between 10 AM and 4 PM the next day
4. If no email available, skip — Levi calls cold but mentions "we may have tried to reach you by email"
5. Log every email sent to the Call Log tab

## LEADS WITHOUT EMAIL
For leads that only have phone numbers (no email):
- Levi calls and leads with: "Hi, this is Levi from Targeted Design Agency. We help {industry} businesses in {ZIP}..."
- No warm-up email needed. Standard cold pitch applies.

## RESULT TRACKING
Track these metrics weekly:
- Emails sent
- Emails opened (if trackable)
- Calls made
- Calls reached (person on phone)
- Appointments set / Interested
- Not interested
-Callbacks scheduled
