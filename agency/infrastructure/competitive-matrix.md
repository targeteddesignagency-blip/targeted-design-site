## 6. Identified Market Gaps & Opportunities

1. **🚫 No Integrated Digital + EDDM Provider** — Biggest gap. Digital agencies and mail shops operate in silos. TDA fills this.
2. **🚫 No Agency Offering EDDM Analytics/Attribution** — Mail shops deliver mail, nobody tracks ROI rigorously. Our analytics differentiation.
3. **🚫 No EDDM + Video Content Combo** — We can pair direct mail campaigns with motion graphics/content marketing. Nobody else does this.
4. **🎯 Affluent Neighborhood Targeting** — RSVP's model proves demand. TDA can replicate with demographic route selection + premium print.
5. **🎯 Military/Veteran Segment** — "Military City USA" angle (EDDM2GO recognizes this). TDA should develop veteran-specific EDDM packages.
6. **⚠️ Postage Rate Increase** — EDDM Retail up 4.9% ($0.223→$0.234). Additional ~4.8% expected July 12, 2026. Combined ~10% increase. Adjust pricing proactively.
7. **⚠️ AI Creative Commoditization** — Free AI tools lowering barriers for basic creative. TDA's competitive advantage is strategy + execution, not just creative output.
8. **🔍 USPS July 2026 Regulatory Watch** — New compliance/promotion/pricing changes expected. Monitor closely for margin impact.
9. **🎯 Remote Competitor Threat** — MPA's dedicated SA landing page signals geographic boundaries weakening. Local presence advantage needs active messaging.
